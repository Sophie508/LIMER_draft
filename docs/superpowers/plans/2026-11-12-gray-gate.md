# Gray-fault benefit gate implementation plan

> Execute with three user-authorized subagents and root integration/review.
> Apply executing-plans checkpoints; unavailable subagent skill packages do not
> replace the native collaboration tools. Publication was explicitly authorized
> after diff delivery on 2026-11-12; further changes require fresh review.

**Goal:** Evaluate an observable switching-benefit policy for sustained slowdown
with continued delivery; retain the direct-recovery throughput release unchanged.

**Architecture:** Add an explicit `benefit_v2` policy beside `always` and legacy
`benefit`. Correct tail and relay-pipeline predictions; release rejected temporary
helper loans causally; account for helper interference through paid observations.
Independent replay and matched policies separate correctness from performance.

**Tech stack:** Native C++/ns-3 simulation, Python standard-library audits, otakotak.

## Global constraints

  versions/2026-11-12-gray-gate. Once registered, a snapshot stays frozen.
  Post-confirmation cleanup repair uses the separate project-terminal-fix copy.
- Preserve all earlier source, evidence, policies and result denominators.
- 16 logical GPUs, four per server; two 10-Gb/s planes, 2400-Gb/s local links;
  64-KiB chunks; four physical NIC credits/helper buffers; source window 32.
- Service detector and thresholds remain frozen in this revision. Detection <100 ms
  is separate from communication completion; no new 95% claim from small pilots.
- Full three-leg probe, health report, 100-Mb/s control FIFO and 1-ms propagation,
  PREPARE/READY/COMMIT and final logical ACK remain charged.
- No hidden injector state or future fault duration in policy. Extra observations
  require a declared, charged, replayable collection path.
- Baselines: adaptive allocation without replacement (G10), direct recovery
  (G11/always), old gate (G11/benefit), candidate (G11/benefit_v2).
- Proposed gray cases: zero-loss sustained and transient rate degradation,
  short/long remaining work; healthy, blackhole, weak loss and constrained helpers
  are regression/boundary controls. Freeze cases and source hashes before running.
- CPU simulation only; no H20; authorized host otakotak. Root alone launches remote
  runs, initially one worker because current research quota has about 25 GiB headroom.
- State exact change and expected observation before every registered campaign.
- Retain every failed/unfinished attempt. Do not describe policy abstractions as
  a reproduction of R2CC; published R2CC already has proxying and schedule models.
